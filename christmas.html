About Our Trini Christmas Favourites

Pastelles are a Trinidad and Tobago Christmas tradition: a seasoned filling of chicken, beef or veggie (soya) wrapped in cornmeal dough and steamed in banana leaf. Ritchie’s pastelles are $45 per dozen.

Black cake is the rich, rum-soaked Caribbean Christmas fruit cake, made the traditional Trinbago way. $35.

Made fresh in Pickering, Ontario, with pickup or delivery for Durham Region and the GTA. Place your order above and we’ll confirm payment and next steps by email. Looking for more? See our full menu, or contact us for catering.
