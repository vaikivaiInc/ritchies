Our Story

The person and the passion behind Ritchie’s.

Cooking has always been my true passion, even while navigating a full-time career in banking and IT. Ritchie’s, formerly Vai-Ki-Vai Inc., carries forward the same spirit of creativity and Caribbean heritage — now with a name that reflects the personal touch behind every recipe.

After moving from Trinidad and Tobago to Canada, I built this brand to share the authentic flavours of home, bringing the comforting taste of Caribbean-inspired treats to a new audience. The original name, rooted in the Trinidadian term “vy-kee-vy” meaning chaotic, captured the lively, unpredictable joy of cooking. That energy still lives here — just under a name that represents me, my story, and the care I put into every product.

At Ritchie’s, we take pride in crafting delicious, homemade delights — from rich Trini sweetbread to bold, flavourful pastries — all made with love and the finest ingredients, true to authentic Caribbean food traditions. Whether you’re craving a nostalgic taste of home or simply looking for something new and exciting, our offerings bring the vibrant essence of the islands straight to your table.

And this is just the beginning. Join us on this delightfully chaotic ride, where authentic flavours, passion, and heritage come together in every bite.
