Christmas Recipe

Trini Chicken Ham

Brine

  • 4 cups water
  • 1.5 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp Prague Powder 1 (ham curing salt)
  • 1 tbsp pickling seasoning
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp liquid smoke

Method

Bring everything to a boil, cool completely, then submerge 4 chicken breasts or 1 whole chicken. Refrigerate 3–4 days.

Glaze

  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ⅛ cup water
  • 1 tsp smoke sauce
  • 1 tsp paprika

Mix until smooth.

Bake & Finish

  1. Remove chicken from brine, pat dry.
  2. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes, basting with glaze throughout.
  3. Broil 2–3 minutes for a glossy, caramelized finish.

Christmas Side Dish

Lemon Potatoes

Serves 6  •  Prep 15 min  •  Cook 55 min

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lbs yellow potatoes
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • 1 cup chicken stock (or water)
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic (about 6 cloves)
  • 1 tbsp chopped chadon beni (culantro) green seasoning
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp mustard

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, stock, garlic, green seasoning, spices, salt, pepper and mustard.
  3. Wash potatoes and cut into wedges. Add to the bowl and toss to coat.
  4. Arrange in a single layer in a baking dish, pour over any remaining liquid, cover with foil and bake 25 minutes.
  5. Remove foil, raise oven to 425°F and roast 15 minutes. Flip and roast 15 more, until tender and golden.
  6. A little liquid will remain in the pan — spoon it over the potatoes when serving.
