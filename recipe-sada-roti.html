Sada Roti

Makes 4 rotis  •  Prep 10 min + 30 min rest  •  Cook 2–3 min each

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • ½ tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp powdered milk (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • About 2 cups warm water (added gradually)

Soft, warm, perfect sada roti.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, powdered milk and salt.
  2. Add water a little at a time until the dough is soft but not sticky.
  3. Cover with a damp cloth and rest 20 minutes.
  4. Knead, divide into 4 pieces and form smooth balls.
  5. Rest 10 minutes.
  6. Heat your tawa or griddle.
  7. Roll each ball to your preferred thickness, using minimal flour.
  8. Cook on the tawa 30 seconds, until small bubbles appear. Flip and cook 30 seconds, then flip again.
  9. Slide the tawa slightly off the heat so the roti is exposed directly to the heat (about 1″ above).
  10. Rotate the roti over the heat until it swells; flip if needed to help it puff.
