Lemon Potatoes

Serves 6  •  Prep 15 min  •  Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lbs yellow potatoes
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • 1 cup chicken stock (or water)
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic (about 6 cloves)
  • 1 tbsp chopped chadon beni (culantro) or green seasoning
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp mustard

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, stock, garlic, chadon beni or green seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper and mustard.
  3. Wash the potatoes and cut into wedges. Add to the bowl with the liquid mixture and toss to coat.
  4. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer in a baking dish, then pour the remaining liquid from the bowl over the potatoes.
  5. Cover with foil and bake in the preheated oven for 25 minutes.
  6. Remove the foil, raise the oven to 425°F, flip the potatoes and roast 15 minutes, or until tender and golden.
  7. A little liquid will remain in the pan — spoon it over the potatoes when serving.
