Rosemary Lemon Roasted Chicken

Serves 4–6  •  Prep 20 min + 3 hrs–overnight marinade  •  Cook 1 hr 15 min+ (until 165°F)

You Need

  • 1 whole chicken (4 lb)

Wet Mix

  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 3 tsp green seasoning
  • 1 tsp liquid smoke

Dry Rub

  • 2 tbsp rosemary
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika

Method

  1. Mix the wet ingredients in one bowl and the dry ingredients in another.
  2. Wash the chicken and pat dry. Coat it all over with the dry rub, inside and out.
  3. Pour the wet mix over the chicken and coat well, inside and out.
  4. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or at least 3 hours.
  5. Roast at 350°F, basting with the pan drippings, until the thickest part of the thigh reaches 165°F.
  6. Finish at 375°F for 10 minutes for a golden skin. Rest 10 minutes, then carve.
