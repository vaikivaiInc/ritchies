Homemade Bread

Makes 4 loaves  •  Prep 20 min + rising (45–60 min, then 45 min)  •  Bake 25–30 min

Ingredients

  • 3½ cups warm water (105°F–110°F so the yeast activates)
  • 2 tbsp yeast
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ⅓ cup honey
  • ⅓ cup oil
  • 8 cups all-purpose flour
  • ⅛ cup powdered milk (optional)
  • 1 tbsp salt

Soft, fluffy, perfect homemade bread.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, powdered milk and salt; set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the warm water, yeast, sugar, honey and oil.
  3. Add the flour mixture 1 cup at a time until the dough is smooth.
  4. First rise: place the dough in a bowl and cover with plastic wrap (not touching the dough), then a hot damp cloth on top. With the oven OFF, put a separate bowl of hot water at the bottom of the oven and place the dough in the oven too. Rest 45–60 minutes, until doubled.
  5. Punch down, knead lightly, divide into 4 equal pieces and place in loaf pans.
  6. Brush the tops with melted butter or oil.
  7. Second rise: return the pans to the oven the same way and rest 45 minutes.
  8. Remove the pans, preheat the oven to 325°F and bake 25 minutes.
  9. For a darker crust, raise to 350°F for 5 minutes.
