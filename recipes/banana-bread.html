Banana Bread

Makes 1 loaf (or 2 small)  •  Prep 15 min  •  Bake 55–65 min

Ingredients

  • 1 stick butter (½ cup, softened)
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt

Moist, soft, perfect banana bread.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon and salt; set aside.
  2. Whisk the milk, eggs and vanilla together.
  3. Mash the bananas until smooth.
  4. Cream the butter and brown sugar just until well combined.
  5. Add the wet mixture and mix.
  6. Add the mashed bananas and mix.
  7. Fold in the dry ingredients gently with a spatula — do not overmix.
  8. Pour into a greased 9×5 loaf pan (or two 1.5-lb pans).
  9. Bake at 350°F for 55–65 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean.
