Trini Pastelles

Makes 4 dozen  •  2 oz dough balls  •  Boil 20 min

Dough

  • 1 pack Promasa corn flour (2 lb / 1 kg)
  • 8 cups warm water
  • 6 oz vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp Maggi Season-Up
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt

Filling

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 lb minced meat (beef or chicken, your choice)
  • 1 tbsp grind garlic
  • 6 tbsp green seasoning
  • ¼ cup Trinidad pimento peppers, chopped
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 16 oz ketchup (about 1 bottle)
  • 1 tbsp browning
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp pepper sauce
  • ½ lb sweet peppers, chopped
  • 8 oz olives, chopped
  • 2 oz capers, chopped
  • 6 oz raisins

You’ll Also Need

  • Banana leaves
  • Oil for the leaves
  • Foil

Prepare the Leaves

  1. Cut the banana leaves into 8×8″ squares, then wash and wipe them dry. Run them over an open flame until soft and pliable.

Make the Filling

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Add the minced meat and cook, stirring, until it burns off its fresh water and the liquid dries up.
  2. Add the garlic, green seasoning, pimento peppers, black pepper and salt. Stir well.
  3. Add the ketchup, browning, mustard and pepper sauce, and let it cook down.
  4. Add the sweet peppers.
  5. Just before you turn off the heat, stir in the olives, capers and raisins. Let it cool.

Make the Dough

  1. Mix the warm water, vegetable oil, Maggi, sugar and salt.
  2. Gradually add the mixture to the Promasa, mixing until it forms a soft dough.
  3. Roll into 2 oz balls.

Assemble & Boil

  1. Oil a leaf, put a dough ball on it and press it flat.
  2. Add 2 tbsp of filling to the centre, fold the leaf over so the dough closes around the filling, then fold in the edges.
  3. Wrap in foil.
  4. Boil in a large pot of water for 20 minutes.

Tip: You’ll have extra filling. It freezes well for your next batch.
