Sponge Cake

Serves 12–16  •  Prep 20 min  •  Bake 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2¾ cups flour
  • ¼ cup powdered milk (optional)
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1¾ cups sugar
  • 2 sticks butter (8 oz / 1 cup)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp vanilla essence
  • 4 eggs

My mother’s recipe — soft, classic, old-school sponge cake.

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 275°F.
  2. Mix the milk and vanilla in a small bowl.
  3. Combine the flour, powdered milk and baking powder in another bowl.
  4. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  5. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well.
  6. Add the dry and wet ingredients alternately, starting and ending with dry.
  7. Grease and flour your pans. The batter (about 8–9 cups) fits one 9×13 pan or two 8-inch rounds.
  8. Bake 30 minutes at 275°F, then raise to 300°F for 10 minutes.
  9. The cake is ready when a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean and dry.
